# Recall Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

## Introduction

Recall ("Recall", "we", "our", or "us") provides note-taking, voice note, journaling, search, and AI-assisted reflection features. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and what choices you have when using the Recall mobile application and related services.

By using Recall, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

## Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to:

- The Recall iOS and Android applications
- Features available inside the application, including note capture, voice notes, AI-assisted summaries, emotion analysis, and billing flows
- Related backend services used to operate the product

This Privacy Policy does not apply to third-party services that you access independently outside Recall.

## Information We Collect

### Account Information

When you create or access an account, we may collect:

- Email address
- Authentication provider identifier
- Basic profile information returned by sign-in providers such as Apple or Google
- Internal user and session identifiers

If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of your direct personal email address.

### Content You Create

When you use Recall, we may process and store content you intentionally create or upload, including:

- Text notes
- Voice note metadata
- Audio files you record or upload
- Folder and tag organization
- Note-derived summaries, classifications, and analysis results

### AI-Generated and Derived Information

To power product features, Recall may generate or store derived data related to your content, such as:

- Summaries
- Suggested tags
- Embeddings or semantic representations
- Retrieval outputs
- Emotion and energy signals
- Thought DNA or similar cognitive pattern summaries
- Daily question or reflection prompts based on your notes

### Billing and Subscription Information

If you purchase a subscription, we may receive subscription-related records such as:

- Entitlement status
- Product identifier
- Store source
- Subscription expiration and renewal status

We do not collect or store your full payment card number. Payments are processed by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, or other authorized billing infrastructure.

### Device and App Information

We may collect limited technical information required to operate the app, such as:

- App version
- Operating system and platform
- Device-generated identifiers
- Crash or diagnostic context
- Notification token where applicable

### Support and Communications

If you contact us, we may collect:

- Your email address
- The content of your support request
- Any attachments, screenshots, or diagnostic details you provide

## How We Use Information

We use information for the following purposes:

- To create and maintain your account
- To authenticate you and keep your session secure
- To store, organize, and display your notes and voice content
- To power AI-assisted product features
- To provide billing, entitlement, and restore-purchase functionality
- To analyze service reliability, performance, and abuse prevention
- To respond to support requests
- To improve the product and user experience
- To comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information.

## AI and Content Processing

Recall includes AI-assisted features. When you use these features, your content may be processed by third-party infrastructure used to deliver:

- Summaries
- Classification
- Retrieval-augmented responses
- Transcription
- Emotion or pattern analysis

We use that processing to operate features you request inside the app. AI outputs may be imperfect, incomplete, or inaccurate and should not be treated as medical, legal, or professional advice.

## Authentication Providers

You may sign in using email/password, Sign in with Apple, or Google Sign-In where supported.

When you authenticate with a third-party identity provider:

- We receive only the information needed to identify your account and operate the sign-in flow
- Your authentication with that provider remains subject to the provider's own privacy practices

## Voice Notes and Audio

If you use voice features:

- Audio you record may be stored in application storage or connected cloud storage
- Audio may be processed for transcription or feature generation
- Metadata related to the recording may be stored to support playback, organization, and analysis

You are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate rights and consent for audio you upload or record.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications:

- We may store a push token associated with your account or device
- We may use notifications to deliver app reminders, including resurfacing or reminder flows available in the product

You can disable notifications in your device settings at any time.

## Billing and RevenueCat

Recall may use RevenueCat or similar subscription infrastructure to manage subscription state and entitlements.

This may include:

- Receiving entitlement updates
- Validating active subscription status
- Restoring purchases
- Storing subscription state on our backend for product access control

Apple or Google may also independently process billing information in accordance with their own policies.

## Sharing of Information

We may share information only as reasonably necessary in the following situations:

- With infrastructure providers that host or support the app
- With authentication providers used for sign-in
- With billing and subscription providers
- With AI service providers needed to perform requested app features
- When required by law, legal process, or governmental request
- To protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud and abuse
- In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, or sale of assets

We do not sell your personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

## Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

- Provide the service
- Maintain your account
- Enforce our agreements
- Resolve disputes
- Meet legal, tax, accounting, and security obligations

If you delete content or request deletion of your account, we will aim to remove or anonymize applicable data within a reasonable time, subject to legal, operational, backup, fraud prevention, and security requirements.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

- Access information about you
- Correct inaccurate information
- Request deletion of your information
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Object to or restrict certain processing
- Request portability where applicable

You may also:

- Update some account information from your sign-in provider or app settings
- Delete notes and content you no longer want stored
- Cancel subscriptions through your Apple App Store or Google Play account settings

## Children's Privacy

Recall is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your country of residence, depending on the infrastructure and service providers used to operate Recall. Where required, we take reasonable steps to apply appropriate safeguards.

## Third-Party Services

Recall may rely on third-party providers such as:

- Supabase
- Apple
- Google
- RevenueCat
- AI or transcription providers

Those providers may process data according to their own privacy terms and policies. We encourage you to review their policies where relevant.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we may update the date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the app or through other reasonable means.
