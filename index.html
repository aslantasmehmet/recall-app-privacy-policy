Account deletion

You can delete your account directly in the app:

  • Open Settings
  • Go to Account & Membership
  • Tap Delete account
  • Confirm deletion

If you cannot access your account, email support@venby.co and include the email address used in Recall.

Subscription note

Deleting your Recall account does not automatically cancel active App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

You must cancel subscriptions from your Apple or Google subscription settings.
